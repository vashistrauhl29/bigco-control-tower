BigCo General Company Policies
================================

COMPANY HOLIDAYS 2026
- New Year's Day: January 1
- Memorial Day: May 25
- Independence Day: July 4
- Labor Day: September 7
- Thanksgiving: November 26-27
- Christmas: December 25

EXPENSE POLICY
- Meal expenses: Up to $50 per day for business travel
- Hotel accommodations: Standard rate up to $200 per night
- Mileage reimbursement: $0.67 per mile
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring expenses
- All receipts must be attached for expenses over $25

REMOTE WORK POLICY
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Core hours: 10am - 3pm local time
- Must be available on Slack during working hours
- Monthly in-person team meetings required

DRESS CODE
- Business casual attire is standard
- Casual Fridays permitted
- Client-facing meetings require business formal

TIME OFF
- Vacation days: 15 days per year
- Sick leave: 10 days per year
- Personal days: 3 days per year
- Time off requests must be submitted 2 weeks in advance
